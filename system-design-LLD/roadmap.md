**The actual reason you can skip most of it:** once you deeply understand Strategy, Factory, Observer, Decorator (your core 4 patterns) through 3-4 real problems, the remaining "exotic" patterns (Bridge, Flyweight, Visitor, Memento, etc.) are just _variations of the same composition-over-inheritance idea_ applied slightly differently. You'll recognize them faster later if you ever need them — but grinding all 48 now is exactly the kind of low-signal, high-effort activity that burns weeks without proportionally raising your interview odds.

Stick to your 11, do them the runnable-code way, and you'll be in solid shape.
